## What's here
- `INDEX.md` — the full census: every title, its creation count, and where its text lives.
- `books/` — full texts, published title by title as they are recovered from the record.
- `renames.md` — the publication rename log (titles held from public use until renamed, per the founder's rules).
- `SEAL.md` — the cross-verification page between this repo and the registry.

- Titles listed in `renames.md` are held from any public use until renamed.
